OFFENSIVE ABILITY / DEFENSIVE ABILITY

Offensive Ability (OA) represents your ability to land effective attacks, while Defensive Ability (DA) represents 
your ability to avoid them. Having higher OA than your target's DA increases your chance to score Critical Hits,
while having lower OA increases your chance to deal Glancing Blows. Likewise, increasing your DA makes enemy 
Critical Hits less likely and Glancing Blows more likely.

Critical Hits deal double damage. If your Critical Hit chance exceeds 100%, the excess becomes a chance to score
a Double Critical Hit for triple damage. If it exceeds 200%, the excess becomes a chance for a Triple Critical Hit 
for quadruple damage, and so on.

Glancing Blows always deal half damage.

If OA and DA are within 10 points of each other, attacks deal normal damage.

Critical Hit Chance: (Attacker OA - Defender DA) / 2
Glancing Blow Chance: (Defender DA - Attacker OA) / 3

For example, an OA advantage of 50 gives 50 / 2 = 25%, or a 1 in 4 chance to Crit.
A DA advantage of 50 gives 50 / 3 = 16.7%, or roughly a 1 in 6 chance for the attack to be a Glancing Blow.
