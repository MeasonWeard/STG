RESISTANCES

Elemental Resistance provides a random flat reduction to incoming damage of the corresponding type. 
Each hit reduces the damage by a randomly selected amount between 20% and 100% of your Resistance, 
rounded down. For example, 50 Fire Resistance reduces incoming Fire damage by 10-50 per hit.

Minimum reduction: Resistance / 5, rounded down
Maximum reduction: Resistance

Negative Resistance represents a vulnerability rather than damage reduction. Incoming damage of the 
corresponding type is increased by the absolute value of your negative Resistance. For example, 
-20 Fire Resistance causes the defender to take 20% more Fire damage.

Extra Damage: Incoming damage X (-Resistance / 100)

Melee and Projectile Resistance are applied after all damage types have been combined, 
reducing the total damage of Melee or Projectile attacks respectively.

Resistance can never reduce incoming damage to 0. An attack will always deal at least 1 damage 
for each elemental damage type it contains.
