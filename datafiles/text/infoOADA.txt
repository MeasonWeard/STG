OFFENSIVE ABILITY / DEFENSIVE ABILITY

Offensive Ability (OA) represents your ability to land effective attacks, while Defensive Ability (DA) represents your ability to avoid them.
Having higher OA than your target's DA increases your chance to score Critical Hits, while having lower OA increases the chance for your attacks
to be Glancing Blows or Dodged entirely. Likewise, increasing your DA makes enemy Critical Hits less likely and increases your chance to reduce
or completely avoid incoming damage.

Critical Hits deal double damage. If your Critical Hit chance exceeds 100%, the excess becomes a chance to score a Double Critical Hit for triple
damage. If it exceeds 200%, the excess becomes a chance for a Triple Critical Hit for quadruple damage, and so on.

Glancing Blows deal half damage. When a Glancing Blow is triggered, the same chance is rolled again. If successful, the attack is Dodged instead
and deals no damage.

If OA and DA are within 10 points of each other, attacks deal normal damage.

Critical Hit Chance: (Attacker OA - Defender DA) / 2
Defensive Outcome Chance: (Defender DA - Attacker OA) / 2

For example, an OA advantage of 50 gives 50 / 2 = 25%, or a 1 in 4 chance to Crit.

A DA advantage of 50 gives 50 / 2 = 25% chance for a defensive outcome. If successful, that 25% chance is rolled again to determine whether the
attack is Dodged instead of becoming a Glancing Blow.
