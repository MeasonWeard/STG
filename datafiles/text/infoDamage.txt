DAMAGE BONUSES

Flat Damage adds a fixed amount of damage to attacks of the corresponding damage type. 
For example, +10 Fire Damage adds 10 Fire damage to an attack.

Damage % increases existing damage of the corresponding type by a percentage. 
For example, +20% Fire Damage increases 50 Fire damage to 60.

Flat Damage is added before percentage bonuses are applied, 
so Damage % also increases any Flat Damage added to the attack.

Damage Multiplier = 1 + (Damage % X 0.01)
Damage = (Base Damage + Flat Damage) X Damage Multiplier

Most skills use these same damage calculations, as do guns and melee weapons.
Gun Damage % and Melee Damage % are applied after other damage bonuses have been calculated.
They multiply the total resulting damage of guns and melee weapons respectively.
