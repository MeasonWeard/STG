RESEARCH

Research projects provide stat upgrades to your character.
You can select only one research project at a time to receive research progress.
Resarch progresses automatically as you collect resources during a run and when scrapping loot.
Collected data and contributing resources are directed towards your selected project instead of your inventory.

You can activate the bonuses of one project from each research category. You will gain the bonuses of each
activated project.

The project you're currently researching does not need to be activated, allowing you to work towards new
upgrades while continuing to benefit from previously completed research.
