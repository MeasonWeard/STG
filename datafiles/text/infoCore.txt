CORE STATS

Health determines how much damage you can take. Damage reduces your Health, and when your Health reaches 0, you lose.

Energy fuels your abilities and is consumed when using skills.

Shields provide protection from incoming attacks. Each Shield point completely negates all damage from a single hit,
regardless of how much damage that hit would have dealt. Some abilities explicitly ignore Shields, allowing their 
damage to pass through and damage your Health without consuming a Shield point.

Healing % increases the amount of Health restored by healing effects. It affects Health recovered from Stim Packs and 
Health Recharges, as well as the amount of Health you restore to yourself and allies with healing abilities.
It does not affect Health Regeneration.
